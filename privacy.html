2. What is HomePoints?

HomePoints is a mobile app for organizing household tasks together in a playful way (tasks, points, streaks, rewards, statistics) in households, shared flats and families. Certain additional features can be unlocked with a paid subscription ("HomePoints Plus").

3. Data we process

Account and profile data

  • a unique, pseudonymous user ID (you start using the app with an anonymous account, without providing personal data)
  • display name and avatar/color (freely chosen)
  • if you voluntarily secure your account with "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google": email address and the provider's account identifier

Household data

  • household membership and invite codes
  • role (e.g. admin), including automatic role transfer when the last admin leaves
  • links to other members

Usage data

  • created, completed and recurring tasks (including optional assignment to members)
  • collected points, streaks and statistics
  • created and redeemed rewards
  • activity timestamps

Purchase and subscription data (only if you use HomePoints Plus)

  • subscription status and term of the household
  • pseudonymous transaction identifiers from the app store
  • we do not process payment data (e.g. credit card details); payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google

Technical data

  • IP address (temporarily, during connections)
  • device information (operating system, app version)
  • push token of the device (for notifications)
  • error and crash logs

Analytics data

  • pseudonymized usage events (e.g. "task completed", "onboarding finished") to improve the product, without task titles, reward texts or profile names (see section 7)

4. Purposes of processing

  • providing the app's features
  • account management and account securing (device change, restore)
  • real-time synchronization between household members
  • points, streak and reward system and statistics
  • handling and managing the Plus subscription
  • sending notifications (e.g. reminders, household activity, weekly recap)
  • pseudonymized analysis of usage to improve the app
  • improving app stability and fixing errors

5. Legal bases

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – performance of a contract (core features, account, subscription handling)
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest (stability, security, pseudonymized usage analysis)
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR – consent (push notifications; you grant permission via your device's system dialog and can revoke it at any time in the device or app settings)

6. Hosting and data processing (backend)

The app uses Supabase Inc. (970 Toa Payoh North, #07-04, Singapore / headquarters USA) as its backend provider.

  • Database and authentication are hosted in the EU region (Frankfurt, Germany) on AWS infrastructure
  • Sub-processor: Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL (Luxembourg)

A data processing agreement (DPA) pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Supabase. As Supabase Inc. is a US company, processing is additionally based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR.

Supabase privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

7. Usage analytics with PostHog

To improve the app we use PostHog (PostHog Inc.). Processing takes place on servers in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).

  • We collect pseudonymized usage events (e.g. app start, task completed, onboarding step) with a random user identifier
  • We do not collect task titles, reward texts, profile names or email addresses
  • There is no cross-device advertising tracking; no advertising IDs (e.g. IDFA) are used

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). A data processing agreement is in place with PostHog. PostHog privacy policy: https://posthog.com/privacy

8. Payment processing and subscription management (HomePoints Plus)

Purchases of the Plus subscription are handled entirely through the app marketplace you downloaded the app from: the Apple App Store (Apple Inc.) or Google Play (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Apple or Google is the contractual partner for the payment; we do not receive any payment data. The privacy policies of Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy) apply.

To manage subscriptions (purchase status, term, restoring purchases) we use RevenueCat Inc. (USA). RevenueCat processes the pseudonymous user ID as well as transaction and subscription data from the app store for this purpose. A data processing agreement (DPA) is in place with RevenueCat; transfers to the USA are based on the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

9. Push notifications

If you allow push notifications, we send you, for example, reminders, updates about activity in your household (such as "<Name> completed a task"), progress updates on rewards and a weekly recap.

  • For delivery we process your device's push token
  • Delivery is handled by the push service of Expo (Expo, Inc., USA) and, depending on your device, the Apple Push Notification service (APNs) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) on Android devices
  • You can disable notifications at any time in the app settings or in your device's system settings

10. Visibility within your household

Data you create in a household (tasks, points, streaks, redeemed rewards, statistics) is visible to all members of that household. This is part of the core functionality. Outside your household, this data is not shared.

11. Disclosure to third parties

There is no disclosure to third parties for advertising purposes, no sale of data and no integration of advertising networks. Data is only passed on to the service providers named in this policy (Supabase, PostHog, RevenueCat, Expo, Apple, Google) to the extent necessary for the respective purpose.

12. Retention period

  • Account and household data: until the account is deleted or you leave the household
  • Analytics data: pseudonymized; not attributed to deleted accounts
  • Purchase/transaction data: as long as required for subscription management or statutory retention obligations
  • Error logs: at most 30 days
  • After account deletion: complete removal within 30 days, unless statutory retention obligations apply

13. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right at any time to:

  • access (Art. 15)
  • rectification (Art. 16)
  • erasure (Art. 17)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • data portability (Art. 20)
  • object (Art. 21)
  • withdraw consent (Art. 7(3))

To exercise these rights, contact: boernerchristian@web.de

14. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time directly in the app:
Settings → Account & Data → Delete account

If you are the last member of your household, the household is deleted completely. If you are the last admin but other members remain, the longest-standing member automatically becomes admin before deletion; the other members' data is retained.

Alternatively by email: boernerchristian@web.de

15. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your residence. Overview for Germany: https://www.bfdi.bund.de/DE/Service/Anschriften/anschriften-node.html

16. Minimum age

HomePoints is intended for persons aged 16 and over. Younger users may only use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian (Art. 8 GDPR).

17. Data security

  • Encrypted transmission (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Encrypted storage in the database
  • Access restriction via Row Level Security (RLS)
  • Write access to points and subscription data exclusively via secured server functions

This policy will be updated in case of functional or legal changes. The current version is available in the app and at the published URL.
